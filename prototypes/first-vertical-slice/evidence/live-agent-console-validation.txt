## Cleanup-review re-run

A later review found three cleanup-seam defects. Each fix was driven by a
failing public-seam test before implementation:

- a containing process identity could pass substring authorization;
- a symlinked ancestor could redirect directory or socket cleanup;
- refused resources were discarded while `clean` incorrectly became true.

The registry now verifies byte-for-byte opaque process identity equality at
registration and cleanup, captures directory/socket device and inode identity,
rejects symlink components at registration and cleanup, retains refused
resources for exact retry, and reports clean only when no registration remains.
