DESIGN DECISION
- Selected: one deep `temporary-panel` target with
  request(versionedJson) -> versionedJson and operations capabilities, register, status,
  summon, call, hide, unregister, inspect. Registration is asynchronous and returns an
  operation identity before a distinct opaque lifecycle identity.
- Rejected: adding six or more shallow lifecycle methods to the existing `shell` target.
  That shape obscures asynchronous registration, duplicates parsing/version/error bounds,
  and risks mixing normal plugin IDs with temporary lifecycle authority.
